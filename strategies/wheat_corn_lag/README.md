# Wheat-corn lag

Systematic version of a discretionary trade: watching `1.5*ZW - ZC` and `ZW - 1.5*ZC` on a 15-30 minute chart.

## LOCKED RULE (2026-11-02)

Locked by the trader: no further optimisation. Code `kalman_rule.py`, settings `config.yaml` -> `kalman_rule`.
Config hash `5721fc9f45` = the walk-forward it was locked on (`runs/20261102-154125_validation`); with the holdout
split added (holdout_end) the hash is `f2543b2ad0` (`runs/20261102-161144_validation`, identical results). Holdout A

| step | rule |
|---|---|
| fair value | Kalman filter, wheat index = alpha + beta x corn index (roll-free 15-min indices), memory ~20 sessions |
| gap | wheat minus the filter's prediction from BEFORE the bar (the live gap) |
| signal s | -(gap / its 20-session RMS around zero); + = wheat cheap vs corn -> buy wheat |
| tradable | only bars Layer 1 scored: not the ~6 sessions after a possible roll window |
| trigger | s in the top / bottom 20% of its past year (past bars only) |
| re-arm | on leaving the tail, at each new session, and every 4.5 h while s stays in the tail |
| minimum | skip a trigger whose gap is < 6 c at entry |
| instrument | wheat OUTRIGHT front month, no corn hedge |
| entry | signal at a 15-min bar's close, fill from the next bar's open |
| positions | at most 2 open at once |
| exits | target when half the gap has closed (limit); safety stop at 2 x the gap; else after 1 session (71 bars) |
| costs assumed | 1.4 ticks (0.35 c) round trip, 1 tick extra on stops |

Evidence (development 2020-01 to 2025-12; cents, 1 tick = 0.25 c, 1 c = $50 per contract):
- Layer 1: rank IC 0.13 at 1 session, t 4.2, 6/6 years, deciles in order; edge strongest in wheat outright.
- Walk-forward out of sample 2021-2025: 683 trades (~2.4 a week), net per trade +2.18 c (t 5.6), win rate 65%,
  average winner +7.9 c / loser -8.3 c, Sharpe 1.74. By year: 2021 -0.40, 2022 +7.56, 2023 +2.47, 2024 +1.49,
  2025 +1.48 c per trade. Gates passed.
- Per contract per trade: ~$15k a year, worst drawdown $8.7k; 99% 5-year bootstrap drawdown $14.6k (scratch sizing:
  ~34 contracts per trade for a $500k drawdown budget; ~$512k a year on average, ~$270k without 2022).
- Capacity (2026 volume): building the entry over 1-2 h at <= 10% of volume fills 34+ lots at every hour except the
  03:00-07:00 London lull (needs 2-3 h).

Known weak spots: 2021 lost in every version; 2022 carries much of the profit; 2024-25 per-trade edge ~1-1.5 c;
fills on 15-min bars assume a full fill at the target level; costs at size are untested live.

Tested and NOT adopted (see the log): spread instead of outright; wait-for-the-turn and limit entries; stops in
cents or ATR; 30% tails; no position limit.

## 0. Hypothesis

### v2 (2026-10-30, current): fade wheat's overshoot of a corn move
Rewritten after Step 5 with the trader's agreement. It came OUT of the development data (Steps 5-5c), so
development results are not evidence for it: only the walk-forward (Step 6) and the holdout (Step 8) count.
- **Thesis:** when the grain complex makes a real move and wheat goes further than its usual ratio to corn,
  wheat gives the excess back over roughly the next session. This is the trader's Case 1 (fade the complex
  move when the spreads diverge), sized properly: estimated beta instead of the fixed 1.5, no cap on how far
  wheat overshot, and only moves big enough to matter. Case 1.1 (short wheat that lagged corn) is dropped.
- **Entry (long or short wheat outright):** over lookback L, corn moved more than X sd AND wheat's own move
  (`own_z`, the part corn does not explain) is more than Y sd in the SAME direction. Trade against that direction.
- **Exit:** triple barrier (profit target, stop, time stop), barriers in wheat volatility units.
- **Why it might pay:** wheat over-reacts to complex-wide flow and the excess is given back; the counterparty
  is whoever chased the move in the more volatile leg.
- **Kill criteria:** walk-forward OOS Sharpe or fold consistency below the gates in `config.yaml`, or the
  edge does not survive 1.4 ticks of costs and a one-bar entry delay.

### v1 (2026-10-30, rejected in Step 5)
- When corn moves and wheat follows by less than usual (it lags), wheat moves against corn next
  (`forward r_W = a*m + b*e` with a < 0, b > 0). Result: b < 0 in all 54 cells (wheat's own move reverses),
  the literal rule F0 earns about 0 bp before costs, and Case 1.1 (short the laggard) -10 bp.
- **Not v3.2:** v3.2 fades wheat when WHEAT led alone, on 20-120 day level fair values. v2 fades wheat only when
  it overshot a CORN move (wheat moving alone does not revert here: -3 bp), on 2h-1 session lookbacks.

## Architecture (agreed with the trader, 2026-10-30)
Pricing and execution are separate questions. Layer 1 builds robust measures of where wheat should trade and
judges them ONLY by statistics (IC on forward returns, pass criteria fixed before each run). Layer 2 decides
why to take a trade now. Nothing reaches Layer 2 without passing Layer 1.

### Layer 1a: signals (each a forecast of wheat OUTRIGHT, the front month; judged by IC at its own horizons)
| Signal | Question | Horizon | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1a wheat vs corn | did wheat overshoot what corn implies? (move fair value, rolling beta, empirical scaling, composite L = 106/142/213) | 2-4 sessions | PASSED (IC 0.145 at 2 sessions, t 3.8); strength 2022+ |
| 1b wheat vs its own curve | has the front priced what the deferreds did? (trader's `2*M1 - M2 - M3`, and M1 vs a rolling M2/M3 fit) | intraday - 2 days | needs contract-level ZW |
| 1c wheat vs world wheat | US wheat vs KC, Matif and world cash / FOB (US competitiveness) | 2-4 days (daily data) | KC + Matif tested 2026-10-30: passes alone (IC 0.13, t 3.3) but = the corn signal (corr 0.78, adds nothing jointly, t 0.8) = wheat alone (0.144). DECISION: NOT a Model 1 signal; world wheat moves to the conditioner layer (US-specific vs global overshoot) and to Model 2 context (CBOT vs Matif in USD = export competitiveness). Revisit only with intraday KC/Matif that add IC jointly. Cash / FOB still to test |
Signals with different information are NOT put into one regression (neighbouring contracts or near-duplicate
factors swamp it: soybeans added nothing to corn). They meet at the forecast level.

### Layer 1b: regime and context (slow fair values; describe, do not time)
| Model | What it is | Use | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 balance-sheet level | wheat price vs U.S. stocks-to-use (WASDE, nominal log-log, elasticity about -1) | regime: price far from its fundamentals = premium that can deflate; context for sizing | built: +-15% out of sample, LAGS price (fair value catches up to price) |
| 2 weekly nowcast | export pace (inspections now; export sales with a key) -> implied stocks | anticipates USDA's revisions (68% direction) -> report-day angle | built; no price edge |
| 2b storage / calendar spreads | spread as % of full carry vs stocks-to-use (Working curve) | the curve's regime and risk: ample stocks = pinned near carry, quiet; tight = free to invert, ~3x the movement; full carry is a hard ceiling | proven on corn 2010-2025; wheat needs ZW contracts + the VSR history |

### Conditioners (measured in Layer 1 as conditional IC; used by the meta model)
- USDA timing: divergence born over a report (IC 0.07 vs 0.16), report ahead (0.25 vs 0.13): not significant yet.
- COT crowding: sell signals work when specs are heavily short (IC 0.22 vs 0.01): confounded with the 2022+ regime.
- World wheat: is the overshoot US-specific (CBOT also beyond KC + Matif) or global? IC 0.24 vs 0.08 (2-3 days),
  same direction 5/5 years at 3 days, but t 0.6-0.8 (186 US-specific days): strongest prior so far, not established.
- Storage regime for the curve signal (1b), once it exists.
- Cash / basis confirmation (below).

### Combining signals
Per horizon, weight each signal by its IC NET of its overlap with the others (a multivariate regression of
forward returns on the signals), fitted walk-forward and shrunk toward equal weights. Two candidate structures,
the data decides: (1) a per-horizon blend; (2) the slower signal (1a) sets the bias, the faster one (1b) times
the entry: trade when the fast signal fires in the slow signal's direction.

### Daily cash prices (e.g. Black Silo: physical cash and FOB from market participants, daily, 20+ years, API)
In a firm setting these are a core input. They feed three places, depending on the price:
- delivery-point and interior BASIS (cash - futures): the storage / curve model (2b). Futures converge to cash
  at delivery, so the nearby spread and the delivery-point basis are tied by arbitrage. It is the daily,
  price-based reading of storage tightness (stocks-to-use is monthly and stale).
- world export cash (US Gulf, Black Sea, EU FOB): signal 1c (US vs world wheat) and the export-demand nowcast
  in Model 2 (an uncompetitive US FOB -> weaker exports).
- basis behaviour as CONFIRMATION (the trader's rule: trade the Russian cash move only if the US curve agreed):
  a conditioner in the meta model.
Point-in-time traps: quote time vs the futures close (cash is often quoted after it), stale quotes on thin
days, quoted locations changing over time. Every quote needs a reliable "known at" timestamp, or basis will
seem to lead futures when it followed them.

### Layer 2: execution
Entries, triple-barrier exits (target 0.5 x divergence, safety stop 2 x divergence, time stop), next-bar-open
fills, sizing by regime (Model 2 / 2b), and the meta model on a deliberately broad primary signal.

### Data needed to finish Layer 1
contract-level ZW (unlocks 1b, 2b on wheat and exact roll stitching); KC and Matif (1c); daily cash / FOB / basis
with timestamps (1c, 2b, conditioners); export sales API key (Model 2 nowcast).
